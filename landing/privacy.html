Niz Khata

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

Niz Khata is a money ledger for Android made by Valorum Labs ("we"), who are responsible for the data described here. This policy explains what the app collects, where it is kept, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: your records are stored so you can use them on your phones and share them with people you choose, and for nothing else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold.

What we collect

From your Google account

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo link. They identify you to the people you share a workspace or shared ledger with.

What you enter

The records you create: workspaces, accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, dues, debts, contacts, notes, and the shared ledger entries you add. Contacts are only the names, phone numbers and email addresses you type in; the app does not read your phone's contact list. Each change is also kept in a history log inside its workspace so members can see who changed what.

What stays on your phone

  • Bank statements you import are read on your phone. The file itself is never uploaded; only the transactions you choose to save are.
  • Statement passwords you ask the app to remember are kept in your phone's encrypted, Keystore-backed storage and are never sent anywhere.
  • App lock uses Android's own fingerprint or screen-lock check. The app never sees your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Reminders and the home-screen widget are prepared on your phone.

Where it is kept

Your records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, part of Google Firebase, and your sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms.

The app also downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your device's network address when it does.

Who can see it

  • You, on any phone where you sign in.
  • People you invite to a workspace, within the role you give them. A role can be limited to viewing, or to one contact's records.
  • The other person in a shared ledger sees the entries between the two of you, with your name and email, and nothing else from your books.

Access is enforced by the database's security rules, not only by the app. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and do not use it for advertising.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet: to sync your records.
  • Notifications: for reminders on the day a due falls.
  • Run at startup: to set reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Biometric: for the optional app lock.
  • Vibrate: for reminder notifications.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a record, a workspace or your account removes it from the database. Google may hold encrypted backups for a limited period before they expire.

Deleting your account

In the app, open More, then Account, then Delete my account. This deletes every workspace you own with all its records, removes you from workspaces others own, deletes the shared ledger entries you created and your profile, and finally deletes your sign-in. See how to delete your account, including how to ask for deletion without the app.

Records in a workspace someone else owns belong to that workspace and stay with its owner. Shared ledger entries the other person created stay in their records.

Children

Niz Khata is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Using Niz Khata is also subject to the terms of use.
