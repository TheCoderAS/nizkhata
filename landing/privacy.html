On this page

01Who we are

Niz Khata is a money ledger for Android made by Valorum Labs ("we"), who are responsible for the data described here.

02What we collect

From your Google account

Your name, email address and profile photo link, so the people you share with know it's you.

What you enter

Your workspaces, accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, dues, debts, contacts, notes and shared ledger entries. Contacts are only what you type; the app never reads your phone's contact list. Changes are kept in each workspace's history so members can see who changed what.

From your phone

For each phone you are signed in on: a push notification token, its time zone and its date format, so reminders reach it at the right local time. It is removed when you sign out there or delete your account.

03What stays on your phone

  • Bank statements you import are read on your phone. Only the transactions you choose to save are uploaded.
  • Statement passwords stay in your phone's encrypted, Keystore-backed storage and are never sent anywhere.
  • App lock uses Android's own fingerprint or screen-lock check. The app never sees either.
  • The home-screen widget is prepared on your phone.

04Where it is kept

In Google Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase), with sign-in by Firebase Authentication, encrypted in transit and at rest, under the Firebase data processing terms. The app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which sees your device's network address when it does.

Our server (hosted on Render) also works on your books on a schedule: it adds the next copy of a repeating due, raises each month's credit card bill, and sends due reminders through Firebase Cloud Messaging. These changes appear in your workspace history as "Niz Khata". It keeps no copy of your records, only a short note of which reminders it sent, for 30 days, so none is sent twice.

05Who can see it

  • You, on any phone where you sign in.
  • People you invite to a workspace, within the role you give them.
  • The other person in a shared ledger: only the entries between you two, with your name and email.
  • Our server, for the scheduled work above, and nothing else.
  • An AI assistant you connect, if you choose to. See below.

Access is enforced by the database's security rules, not only by the app. We never sell, rent or share your data, or use it for advertising.

06AI assistants (optional)

You can connect Niz Khata to an AI assistant such as Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI). Nothing is shared unless you connect one yourself.

  • What it can do: read and change your records in the workspaces you belong to, within your role there, when you ask it to.
  • Where your data goes: what the assistant reads is sent to that assistant's provider, and their privacy policy covers it from then on.
  • Our connector (hosted on Render) only passes requests between the assistant and the database. It stores none of your records. Your sign-in travels inside an encrypted token that the assistant holds.
  • Changes it makes appear in the workspace history under your name.
  • To stop, disconnect Niz Khata in the assistant's settings. A connection unused for 90 days signs out by itself.

07Permissions

  • Internet, to sync your records.
  • Notifications, for the reminders our server sends on the day a due falls.
  • Biometric, for the optional app lock.
  • Vibrate, for reminder notifications.

08How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a record, a workspace or your account removes it from the database; Google's encrypted backups expire after a limited period.

09Deleting your account

In the app: More, then Account, then Delete my account. It removes every workspace you own and everything in it, your place in others' workspaces, your shared entries and profile, and finally your sign-in. More on deleting your account, including without the app.

Records in a workspace someone else owns stay with its owner, and shared entries the other person created stay in their records.

10Children

Niz Khata is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children under 13.
