Connect in four steps.

1

Open Connectors

On claude.ai or the Claude desktop app, open Settings, then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.

2

Paste the address

Name it Niz Khata and paste the server URL below.

3

Sign in with Google

Choose Connect and sign in with the same Google account you use in the app.

4

Ask away

Start a chat, make sure Niz Khata is switched on in the tools menu, and say what you need.

Safe by design

It acts as you. Only you.

  • Your access, nothing more. Claude works in your workspaces, within the role you have there. The same security rules as the app apply to every request.
  • Settings stay yours. Claude can read members, roles, workspace settings and your account, but never change them.
  • It records money, never moves it. No bank connection, no payments, no transfers of real money.
  • Asks before deleting. Anything destructive is confirmed first, and deleted records can be restored.
  • Every change is in your history, under your name, just like an entry made on your phone.
  • Nothing kept on our side. The connector passes requests through and stores none of your records. What Claude reads is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Disconnect any time in Claude's settings. An unused connection signs itself out after 90 days.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

The Niz Khata connector is free. Custom connectors depend on your Claude plan and settings; if you don't see "Add custom connector", check Claude's help for your plan.

I have more than one workspace.

Claude asks which one you mean the first time, then stays with it for the conversation until you name another.

Can my family members use it?

Yes, each with their own Google account. Each person sees and changes only what their role in a workspace allows.

Which currencies and dates does it use?

Each workspace's own currency and time zone, the same ones you see in the app. Amounts are never converted.

Where can I read more about privacy?

See the AI assistants section of our privacy policy, or write to hello@valorumlabs.com.
