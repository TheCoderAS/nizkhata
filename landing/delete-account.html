NizKhata

Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open NizKhata and sign in.
  2. Go to More, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, and confirm with Google sign-in.

Deletion happens straight away.

Without the app

Email hello@valorumlabs.com from the Google account you sign in with, asking for your account to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Every workspace you own, with all its accounts, transactions, dues, debts, contacts, budgets, categories and history.
  • Your membership of workspaces other people own.
  • Shared ledger entries you created, your shared connections, and invitations you sent.
  • Your profile (name, email and photo link) and your sign-in.
  • What the app keeps on your phone: remembered statement passwords, reminders and settings.

What is kept

  • Records in a workspace someone else owns belong to that workspace and stay with its owner.
  • Shared ledger entries the other person created stay in their records.
  • Google may hold encrypted backups for a limited period before they expire.

See the privacy policy for more. NizKhata is made by Valorum Labs.
