Delete your account.

Two ways to do it, and exactly what happens to your records.

Instant

In the app

  1. 1Open Niz Khata and sign in
  2. 2Go to More, then Account
  3. 3Tap Delete my account, type DELETE, confirm with Google
Within 30 days

Without the app

Email us from the Google account you sign in with. We delete it and reply to confirm.

hello@valorumlabs.com

What is deleted

  • Every workspace you own, with all its records and history
  • Your membership of workspaces others own
  • Shared entries you created, connections and invitations
  • Your profile and your sign-in
  • What the app keeps on your phone

What is kept

  • Records in workspaces someone else owns stay with their owner
  • Shared entries the other person created stay in their records
  • Google's encrypted backups expire after a limited period
